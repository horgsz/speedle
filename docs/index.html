How to play

Find the four-letter word in six guesses before the 30-second perimeter clock runs out.

  • 30-second clock: Starts on your first letter. Every valid guess adds +3.0 seconds back to the border.
  • Speed score: Row multiplier × (Seconds left × 10), up to 1,800 points.
  • Row color spectrum: Earlier solves lock in a brighter cyan-mint shade:

2nd5×
3rd4×
4th3×
5th2×
6th1×
  • S Right spot — takes that row's green shade.
  • P In the word, different spot.
  • D Not in the word.
